What we tried, and what we did not ship

The gaps above look like a model that is over-confident, and the standard fix is to rescale its probabilities. We tested three candidates: Platt scaling, an adaptive home-ice correction, and fitted ensemble weights. Each one was tuned on the first three test seasons, then applied once to a fourth season it had never seen.

All three gained about 0.0004 log loss on the seasons they were tuned on, and none of it survived on the held-out season. That is the signature of fitting noise, so none of them were shipped. The real finding was that accuracy slid from 59.6% to 55.4% across the four seasons as the model aged, which is a case for retraining and for this page, not for a post-hoc patch. The experiment is reproducible: model/evaluate_model.py.
